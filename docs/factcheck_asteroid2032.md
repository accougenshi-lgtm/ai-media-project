# Fact-check: "Will an Asteroid Hit Earth in 2032?" (idea #18), checked 2026-11-25
- Asteroid: 2024 YR4. Earth impact in 2032 ruled out by NASA (Feb/Mar 2025): "no significant threat to Earth in 2032 and beyond".
  https://blogs.nasa.gov/planetarydefense/2025/02
- Moon: impact odds peaked ~4.3% (mid-2025). JWST data (Feb 2026) ruled lunar impact out; passes the Moon at ~13,200 miles / >20,000 km on 22 Dec 2032.
  https://www.esa.int/Space_Safety/Planetary_Defence/Asteroid_2024_YR4_will_not_impact_the_Moon ; https://www.sciencealert.com/its-official-nasa-confirms-asteroid-2024-yr4-will-fly-past-the-moon
- Honest answer for the Short: No. It once had the highest odds ever recorded for an asteroid its size, then the Moon was next in line, and now both are ruled out.
