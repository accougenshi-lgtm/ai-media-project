## @blakeryan052: what he actually teaches (from transcripts)
He sells YouTube-automation coaching ("students", "clients"). **His income screenshots are self-reported and unverified.**
Recurring claims across his top videos:
1. **"Ultra long form" (2–11 hour videos) is the money format.** Claimed RPMs $20–50 vs $3–5 for normal faceless and $0.10–0.30 for Shorts.
   Examples he cites: sleep channels, "boring" gameplay compilations (3–11 h Black Ops Zombies), anime what-if stories (30–60 min+).
2. Small channels earning big: "6,000 subs, 736K views, ~$30 RPM"; "2,500 subs, $10K from 200K views".
3. AI handles most tasks, **but he says humans still hand-hold the APIs/AI**, even at 25 channels.
4. Method: copy a proven channel's *style* (not its content), pick niches by RPM/demographic, not by topic.

**Independent check (YouTube data, today):** "science to fall asleep to" (this year) median **132,921 views**, videos 2.5–8 h.
Sleepy Science Channel: 364K subs, median 55K/video, avg length 179 min; "8 Hours of Facts About The Universe" 408,535 views.
→ The ultra-long sleep format is **real and works in our exact niche**. The RPM claims remain unverified.

## @sina.growthtech: what works for him
Face-to-camera personality, AI/tech news + hot takes. Best performers:
- Tool tutorials with a listicle hook: "Top 3 Commands To Tell ChatGPT To 10x Your Results" **2.1M**, "How To Use AI To Create TikToks" 1.6M (2023-era).
- Contrarian hooks: "Stop listening to Trump…" 1.5M, "The power grid is not shutting off… stop believing this" 1.3M, "the REAL REASON Jensen Huang…" 607K.
- Hook pattern: first sentence = the claim/promise, no intro, then numbered points, then CTA "follow for more".
**Don't copy:** conspiracy framing, swearing and political takes are advertiser-unfriendly and need a face/personality to work.
**Do copy:** zero-intro hooks, numbered structure, contrarian "the real reason" framing (applied to physics, not politics).

## What changes in our plan
1. **Add a "Science to fall asleep to" ultra-long format (2–3 h).** This is the strongest weapon for the watch-hours goal:
   *estimate:* one 3-h video with 20K views at ~40 min average watch time = **~13,000 watch hours**, over 3× the current 4,000-hour bar on its own.
2. Calm narration matters for sleep; the "dark" voice stays for Shorts/what-ifs, and a softer preset is tested for sleep videos.
3. Engine needs a low-motion mode (slow pans, ~10 fps) so 3-hour renders take hours, not days.
4. Shorts hooks: open on the claim ("The Moon is leaving us. 3.8 cm a year."), numbered beats, "follow for the next one".
