## Read closely (qualitative)
**Sleepless Historian (sleep, 985K):** dry, gentle **humour** throughout ("corpse chic glow… and as a bonus slowly poisoned you").
The first minute holds a soft CTA *plus* a comment prompt: "let me know where you're tuning in from and what time it is for you."
A cozy setup ("dim the lights, maybe turn on a fan"), then "let's ease into tonight's journey together."

**Sleepy Science Channel (sleep, 129K):** a mood-setting intro, a CTA inside the first minute, "But for now, just breathe… Let's begin."
**Modular structure:** each section opens with a one-line fact headline ("Some snowfalls contain microscopic particles from space."), then 4–6 calm sentences unpacking it.
Music bed under everything.

**MrBallen (scary storytelling, 2.2M):** the opening promise names the payoff ("three extremely haunting stories about people who witnessed death up close").
A quirky, memorable CTA ("offer the like button some pistachio ice cream… with wasabi").
Every story opens with **when + where + who** ("Late one night in 1870, a Harvard medical student named James Adams…").
He withholds the reveal and ends each story on **one short twist line** ("…dressed exactly like a 19th century undertaker.").
Conversational asides ("you know", "like"). The end screen recommends **one specific** video, not "check out my channel".

**Fascinating Horror (dark documentary, 1.0M):** a cold open with date + place + foreshadowing ("very few of the guests… were aware that the building had several hidden faults").
Restrained, formal tone. A dry closing joke ("…currently remains without a sequel"). Note: this one is a tongue-in-cheek "documentary" of the *fictional* Towering Inferno.

**Kurzgesagt (informative, 12.8M):** opens with **three questions in five seconds**, then answers immediately ("Well, it sort of does.").
**One concrete analogy per hard idea** (ice cream filling the universe; an ant on an orange).
Honest and self-aware ("we have to simplify and lie a bit here", "If you find this confusing, we are with you", "(sighs) It's time to stop now").
Uses "we" (narrator and viewer together).

## Rules for our scripts from now on
**Sleep videos**
1. Length is the #1 lever: target 90–150 min (render ahead of time in the 10 AM session).
2. First 60 s: a cozy setup, a soft CTA, and the comment prompt "tell me where you're listening from tonight."
3. Modular: a fact headline, then 4–6 slow sentences. Each module stands alone, since listeners drift in and out.
4. Light, dry humour every few minutes (gentle, never loud). Keep stakes low and avoid cliffhangers.
5. Keep ~130 wpm, ~11–17-word sentences, longer pauses between modules; soften toward the end.

**Shorts / what-ifs**
1. Line 1 is a question or a specific promise. Answer it partly in line 2 to earn line 3.
2. One analogy per number (a basketball and a tennis ball beat "27% of Earth's diameter").
3. Hold back the biggest number or twist for the last 5 seconds; end on one short line.
4. Use "we" and be honest about simplifications, which also builds trust in comments.
5. One memorable, slightly odd CTA line is allowed (a personality signal), but no more than one.

**Not copying:** no "you know/like" filler (it sounds wrong in TTS), no sponsor reads, no fictional events presented as real.
