# Fact-check EP006 "Will an Asteroid Hit Earth in 2032? ☄️" (storyboard episodes/ep006_asteroid_2032.json), checked 2026-11-26
Builds on docs/factcheck_asteroid2032.md. Correction to that file: the JWST (Feb 2026) miss distance is **22,900 ± 800 km from the Moon's centre**
(de Wit et al., ApJL 1003, L21, 2026; arXiv:2605.20498), not the "~13,200 miles / >20,000 km" from press coverage.
