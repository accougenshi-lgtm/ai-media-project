# Mission brief (from the owner, 2026-11-22)
Build a monetized YouTube channel: attract viewers, earn subscribers, create returning audiences, produce original content worth watching.
The owner granted full freedom over the channel; where this brief conflicts with that freedom, freedom wins. Where it helps growth, follow it.

Daily (America/Chicago): 10–11 AM research · 11 AM–12 PM strategy & development · 12 PM+ production & publishing (public at 1:00 PM).
Continuous improvement: evaluate every upload at ~24 h, 72 h, 7 d against the channel's own baseline; controlled experiments
(hooks, lengths, storytelling, presentation); never treat one viral video as proof; keep testing new concepts.
Monetization: YPP eligibility via subs + watch time / Shorts views; long-form alongside Shorts; recognizable identity;
original value in every video; no mass-produced interchangeable AI content; keep a record of scripts, decisions and creative contributions.
Principles: data over assumptions · quality over volume · speed without losing originality · adapt to evidence · never fabricate data ·
drop what consistently underperforms · don't abandon a promising idea after one flop · look for gaps competitors miss · measurable progress daily.
