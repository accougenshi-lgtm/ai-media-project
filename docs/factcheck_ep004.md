# EP004 fact check: The Moon is leaving us (Short)
| Claim | Basis |
|---|---|
| Moon recedes ~3.8 cm/yr | Lunar Laser Ranging (NASA; APOLLO/LLR results) |
| ≈ fingernail growth | fingernails ~3–4 cm/yr (~3.5 mm/month); worded "about as fast" |
| Apollo astronauts left mirrors | retroreflectors on Apollo 11, 14, 15 (plus Soviet Lunokhod 1/2) |
| Laser round trip ~2.5 s | 2 × 384,400 km / c ≈ 2.56 s |
| 600 Myr × 3.8 cm = ~22,800 km "at today's rate" | arithmetic; rate not constant over geologic time, hence "at today's rate" |
| Moon will become too small to cover the Sun; last total eclipse ~600 Myr away | commonly cited estimate; script avoids a precise date ("already on its way") |
